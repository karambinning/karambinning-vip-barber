Norfolk Street · King's Lynn town centre

A neighbourhood barbershop — not a chain, not a salon, not a quick-stop. Just an honest cut for men, lads and the young ones.

VIP Barber sits on Norfolk Street in the heart of King's Lynn, a short walk from the bus station and the Tuesday Market. The chair has been there long enough to know the regulars by name and short enough to still welcome a first-timer through the door.

Walk in, take a seat, point at a photo if it's easier — or just say "the usual" if you've been before. We'll get you sorted.
